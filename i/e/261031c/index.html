The Idea

Three photos. Overnight AI. One live dashboard.

No questionnaires. No training. No disruption to the expo floor. Attendees simply photograph what genuinely catches own attention — and the aggregate becomes a real piece of market research.

01
Oct 30 · 11 AM - 7:30 PM

Shoot

Attendees visit the expo whenever suits them during the day. Each captures 3 things they find interesting with a short note on each. Nam Kwong provides bus transport if needed.

02
Oct 30 · Overnight

Analyze

The AI pipeline classifies every photo — colour, car part, brand tier, description language — and builds the dashboard. The software is already built and ready to run.

03
Oct 31 · 2:45 PM

Showcase

Results go live on an ultra-wide LED wall, including a realtime analysis demo. Optional attendance — open to anyone curious to see what their photos revealed.

AI finds
Red beats specs

23% of “interesting” photos were red cars — versus only ~8–10% of cars actually on display.

AI finds
The face wins

Grille + headlights accounted for 40% of attention. People judge cars the way they judge faces.

AI finds
EVs over-index

27% of interest came from roughly 15% of floor space — novelty plus design beats price tag.

The Assignment

Three photos. That's it.

There is no questionnaire to fill in and no preparation required. The instruction is deliberately open — “interesting” is exactly what we want to capture.
