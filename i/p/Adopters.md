## Program structure · multi‑year pathway

Students in Years 1–4 can join. Everyone starts at foundation level; returning students advance each year.

**🔍 Explore** Generative AI, online business models, tech & social entrepreneurship careers

**🧩 Create** Define a social mission, fundraising, networks, business models

**🚀 Launch** Build a website, use AI for operations, working enterprise prototype

## We are looking for students who are…

- **Curious** — ask questions, want to understand how things work
- **Driven** — motivated to build something meaningful
- **Ready to compete** — keen to enter international competitions
- **Pioneers** — inaugural program; students help shape its future
